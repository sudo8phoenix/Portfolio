01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed solving.

Site reports describe work in natural language. Construction schedules use structured activities, units and versions. Connecting them requires preserving the source, resolving ambiguous activity names and separating a model’s suggestion from an authorized change.

03 / ENGINEERING

The decisions behind it.

Keep approval outside the model

FastAPI persists the report and a durable job. LangGraph extracts observations and proposes matches, but the review service owns approval. The agent cannot silently turn a guess into official progress.

Make work recoverable

PostgreSQL stores jobs and checkpoints. Leases, bounded retries and idempotent persistence support recovery without duplicating a proposal after an interrupted run.

Retain source and schedule context

Source locators and versioned schedules travel with observations. Corrections and retractions preserve history. Native schedule file imports are distinct from live vendor synchronization.

Separate progress from delivery

Approved progress can be exported as CSV or JSON. A transactional outbox handles retries and receipts to a mock PMIS adapter. This delivery path is explicitly a prototype.

04 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Claims with context.

120Selections in the documented role rehearsal
118 / 120Declared identity / abstention targets matched

The 26 October 2026 role rehearsal covered 16 worker pairs and ranked 64 composed assignments. All 120 proposals passed validation; 118 met the declared targets. These are single-report component results, not 64 independent graph runs or a production accuracy score. The repository also separates deterministic software tests from live-model studies.

Current limits & next validation

Production acceptance is incomplete. Representative reviewed data, a frozen assembled configuration, fresh validation and holdout, and sustained capacity checks remain open. Earlier assisted graph runs exceeded the 120-second readiness target. Scanned PDFs require reviewed transcription; image reports and automatic OCR are unsupported.
