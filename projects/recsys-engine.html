03 / ENGINEERING

The decisions behind it.

Offer multiple recommendation paths

Content features and KMeans clustering provide different ways to find related titles. Annoy is optional, with feature flags and lower-memory fallbacks.

Cache repeated queries

In-memory and shelve-based persistent caches reduce repeated computation. JSON fallback and indexed title lookups support repeat searches.

Make resource constraints explicit

A fast-start option loads a subset of the data. Feature flags can disable expensive content-based or approximate-search paths on smaller machines.
