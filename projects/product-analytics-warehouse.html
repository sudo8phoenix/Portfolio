What needed solving.

03 / ENGINEERING

The decisions behind it.

Define the grain before the chart

Separate event facts, order facts and identifiable users. A transaction with multiple item events counts once as an order. Missing session IDs in Retailrocket mean a visitor funnel, not a session funnel.

Make runs reproducible

The local standard-library Python pipeline validates inputs and upserts stable event identities. The synthetic fixture is clearly separated from authentic Retailrocket and GA4 analysis.

Check local and warehouse agreement

The same Retailrocket rows were loaded into BigQuery. Nine SQL views implement facts, dimensions, daily activity, week-one retention and the ordered visitor funnel; documented results agree with the local run.

04 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Claims with context.

2,756,101Authentic Retailrocket event rows
1,407,580Visitors in the documented analysis

The source spans 3 May–18 September 2015 UTC and contains 17,672 distinct transaction IDs. Of 1,404,179 visitors who viewed an item, 32,934 subsequently added to cart. A complete-week cohort starting 4 May had 3,425 of 71,389 visitors return in the following calendar week (4.80%). These are dataset findings, not business improvements caused by the project.

Current limits & next validation

Retailrocket does not provide checkout events, session IDs, prices, revenue or acquisition channels. None are inferred. The funnel can span products and sessions across the observation period. BigQuery sandbox tables may expire; checked-in reports preserve the evidence.
